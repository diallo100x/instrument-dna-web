Performance · articulation layers

Poly preserves the original overlapping playback. Mono hands one note to the next; Legato glides and softens the new attack when notes overlap. Release the latest key while holding another to return to it. For recorded slices, Legato makes a short crossfade and pitch glide into the next slice; it cannot recreate an unrecorded breath transition. Tag a source slice as Trill to create the layer. Play Trill manually or enable the velocity switch for notes at or above the threshold (the screen keyboard plays at velocity 100). Recorded trills are reused near their captured pitch; farther keys use a clearly labeled generated trill. Raw always plays the original recording.

Scalar Guitar · performance and hardware

Slide time uses Legato glide on Performance (10–2000 ms). Drive is the main Drive macro; velocity is on Instrument map. Existing articulations apply across E2–E6. In Legato mode overlapping Physical guitar notes retain one voice and its envelope. Pickup profiles are authored tone settings, not analyzed pickup measurements. Original Scalar Guitar remains a separate factory preset.

Physical model · excitation and body

Analyze and review notes, then fit a physical model. Original Hybrid remains available for comparison. Factory instruments are authored designs; fitted instruments retain measured frequencies and decay. Wind excitation and body separation are not fitted. Velocity changes excitation and upper-mode energy. Existing source models retain their original playback modes.
